# Heart of The Alien

*Heart of the Alien: Out of This World Parts I and II* (Sega CD, 1994) was
developed by Interplay Productions and published by Virgin Interactive
Entertainment. It is the sequel to *Another World* by Éric Chahi.

## Heart of The Alien Redux

- Gil Megidish <gilm@users.sourceforge.net> — original Redux engine (2004-2005)
- M-HT — contributions to the original engine
- carstene1ns — 2.0.0 SDL3 port and upstream maintenance
- Jorge Salgueiro — 2.0.1 fork (HD renderer, smoothing, key bindings, fixes)

The 2.0.1 fork was made with the help of opencode and DeepSeek.
